Folio for iPad Support

Folio (页间) is a handwriting notebook for iPad and Apple Pencil, with an AI tutor, Professor Page, who teaches on a whiteboard. It is made by individual developer Li Ming wang.

Setting up the AI

Folio’s AI features use a service you choose, with your own API key. Open the sidebar › AI Settings › Add Service, pick a provider (such as OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, OpenRouter or DeepSeek) and paste the key you created on its website. The provider bills your usage directly. Folio sends content straight from your iPad to that provider and asks before the first time.

Folio Pro

  • Whiteboard lessons, AI Chat, and Explain & Translate need a Folio Pro subscription. Notes, handwriting, recording and iCloud sync are free.
  • On a new or reset iPad: sidebar › Settings › Restore Purchases.
  • To cancel: sidebar › Settings › Manage Subscription, or iPad Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

iCloud Sync

Turn it on in sidebar › Settings › iCloud Sync on each iPad, signed in to the same Apple Account. The first sync of large notebooks can take a while, so keep Folio open on Wi‑Fi. AI settings and API keys stay on each iPad and don’t sync.

Language

Folio follows your iPad’s language: English, Simplified Chinese or Traditional Chinese. To use a different language for Folio only, go to sidebar › Settings › Language. The language Professor Page answers in is set in AI Settings.

页间 iPad 版支持

页间是为 iPad 和 Apple Pencil 做的手写笔记本，里面有一位会在白板上讲题的 AI 老师「页老师」。由个人开发者 Li Ming wang 开发。

设置 AI

页间的 AI 功能用的是你自己选的服务和 API Key。打开侧栏 › AI 设置 › 添加服务，选一个服务商（比如 DeepSeek、通义千问、OpenAI、Claude、Gemini），粘贴你在它网站上创建的 Key。用量费用由服务商直接收取。内容从 iPad 直接发给这个服务商，第一次使用前页间会先征得你的同意。

页间 Pro

  • 页老师白板讲解、AI 聊天、解释与翻译需要订阅页间 Pro。笔记、手写、录音和 iCloud 同步都免费。
  • 换了新 iPad 或重装后：侧栏 › 设置 › 恢复购买。
  • 取消订阅：侧栏 › 设置 › 管理订阅，或者 iPad 的「设置 › 你的名字 › 订阅」。在当前周期结束前至少 24 小时取消，就不会再扣费。
  • 退款由 Apple 处理：reportaproblem.apple.com。

iCloud 同步

在每台 iPad 上打开侧栏 › 设置 › iCloud 同步，并登录同一个 Apple 账户。笔记本很大时第一次同步要一段时间，请连着 Wi‑Fi 并让页间开着。AI 设置和 API Key 只留在各自的 iPad 上，不同步。

语言

页间跟随 iPad 的语言：英文、简体中文或繁體中文。要单独给页间换语言，打开侧栏 › 设置 › 语言。页老师用什么语言回答，在 AI 设置里选。
