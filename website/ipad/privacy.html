Folio for iPad Privacy Policy

Updated October 9, 2026

Folio (页间) is a handwriting notes app for iPad, developed and provided by individual developer Li Ming wang. This policy explains what information Folio handles, where it goes, and how you control it.

In short: Folio has no servers of its own. The developer never receives or sees your notes or what you send when you use AI.

Your notes

  • Notebooks, handwriting, imported PDFs and images, recordings and whiteboards are stored on your iPad.
  • When you turn on iCloud Sync, they are stored in your own private iCloud database, kept by Apple and visible only to devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The developer can’t access it. When you turn sync off, nothing more is uploaded and your existing notes stay as they are.
  • Exports and backups (.folio files, PDFs, images) happen only when you make them, and are saved where you choose.

AI features

  • The AI features — Professor Page’s whiteboard lessons, AI Chat, and Explain, Translate and Ask on selected text — use the service you choose in AI Settings (such as OpenAI, Claude, Gemini or DeepSeek) with your own API key.
  • When you use an AI feature, Folio sends what you are asking about directly from your iPad to the service you chose. This can include selected or framed PDF text and screenshots, recognized handwriting, note content, your questions, and your speech as text. Nothing passes through any server of the developer’s.
  • Before you use a service for the first time, Folio tells you where the content will go and asks for your permission. You can withdraw it in Settings › Folio Pro; you will then be asked again the next time.
  • How these services handle what you send is governed by each provider’s own privacy policy. Please read it before use.
  • If you connect to a model running on your own computer on the same Wi‑Fi (Ollama, LM Studio), the content stays within your own network.
  • API keys are stored in this iPad’s keychain. They don’t sync to iCloud and are never sent anywhere except to their own service.

Voice

  • Class recordings are kept in their notebooks, under the same rules as your notes.
  • When you talk with Professor Page, what you say is turned into text — on the iPad where the device supports it, otherwise by Apple’s speech recognition service. The text is sent as your question to the AI service you chose.
  • If you choose an online speech service in AI Settings (such as Qwen TTS, Doubao Speech or MiniMax) for the professor’s voice, the text the professor speaks is sent to that service to make the audio.

Subscriptions and payment

The Folio Pro subscription is bought through the App Store, and payment is handled by Apple. The developer receives only aggregate sales data from Apple, never your name, Apple Account or payment details. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your iPad’s Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

What we don’t do

  • No sign-up, and no collection of personal information such as your name, phone number or email address.
  • No ads, no cross-app tracking, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We don’t sell or rent any data.

Permissions

  • Microphone: recording classes and talking with Professor Page.
  • Speech recognition: turning what you say to Professor Page into text.
  • Camera: photographing problems and scanning documents into your notes.
  • Local network: connecting to models running on a computer on the same Wi‑Fi.

The system asks for each permission the first time it is needed, and you can turn any of them off at any time in your iPad’s Settings › Folio.

Children

Folio does not knowingly collect anyone’s personal information. Before using AI features, minors should choose an AI service and read its privacy policy with the consent and guidance of a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, this page is updated along with the date above; significant changes are noted in the app’s release notes.

页间 iPad 版隐私政策

更新于 2026 年 10 月 9 日

页间是一款 iPad 手写笔记应用，由个人开发者 Li Ming wang 开发和提供。这份政策说明页间会接触哪些信息、这些信息去哪里、你可以怎样控制它们。

简单说：页间没有自己的服务器，开发者收不到、也看不到你的笔记和你用 AI 时发送的内容。

你的笔记

  • 笔记本、手写、导入的 PDF 和图片、录音、白板记录，都存在你这台 iPad 上。
  • 打开「iCloud 同步」后，这些内容会存进你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库，由 Apple 保管，只有登录同一个 Apple 账户的设备能看到，开发者无法访问。关掉同步后不再上传，已有的笔记不受影响。
  • 导出、备份（.folio 文件、PDF、图片）只在你主动操作时进行，保存到你选择的位置。

AI 功能

  • 页老师白板讲解、AI 聊天、解释、翻译、提问等 AI 功能，使用的是你自己在「AI 设置」里选择的服务（例如 DeepSeek、通义千问、OpenAI 等）和你自己的 API Key。
  • 使用 AI 功能时，页间会把这次要问的内容从你的 iPad 直接发送给你选择的服务，可能包括：选中或框选的 PDF 文字和截图、手写识别出的文字、笔记内容、你的提问、语音转成的文字。内容不经过开发者的任何服务器。
  • 第一次使用某个服务前，页间会告诉你内容将发往哪里，并征得你的同意。你可以在「设置 › 页间 Pro」里撤回同意，之后再使用时会重新询问。
  • 这些服务如何处理你发送的内容，由各服务商自己的隐私政策决定，请在使用前阅读。
  • 如果你连接的是同一 Wi-Fi 下自己电脑上运行的模型（Ollama、LM Studio），内容只在你自己的网络里传输。
  • API Key 保存在这台 iPad 的钥匙串里，不会同步到 iCloud，也不会发给除对应服务以外的任何地方。

语音

  • 课堂录音保存在对应的笔记本里，规则与笔记相同。
  • 和页老师语音对话时，你说的话会被转成文字：设备支持时在 iPad 上本地识别；不支持时由 Apple 的语音识别服务处理。转成的文字作为提问发给你选择的 AI 服务。
  • 如果你在 AI 设置里选择了在线语音服务（如通义百炼、豆包语音、MiniMax）为老师配音，老师要说的文字会发送给该服务合成声音。

订阅与付款

页间 Pro 订阅通过 App Store 购买，付款由 Apple 处理。开发者只能从 Apple 得到汇总的销售数据，拿不到你的姓名、Apple 账户或付款信息。订阅可以在 iPad 的「设置 › Apple 账户 › 订阅」中管理或取消。

我们不做的事

  • 不要求注册账户，不收集姓名、电话、邮箱等个人信息。
  • 没有广告，不做跨应用追踪，不使用第三方统计或广告 SDK。
  • 不出售、不出租任何数据。

权限

  • 麦克风：课堂录音、和页老师语音对话。
  • 语音识别：把你对页老师说的话转成文字。
  • 相机：拍题目、扫描文稿放进笔记。
  • 本地网络：连接同一 Wi-Fi 下电脑上运行的模型。

每项权限都在第一次需要时由系统询问，你可以随时在 iPad 的「设置 › 页间」里关闭。

未成年人

页间不会有意收集任何人的个人信息。未成年人使用 AI 功能前，请在监护人同意和指导下选择 AI 服务并阅读其隐私政策。

政策更新

政策有变化时会更新本页面并修改上方日期；重大变化会在应用更新说明中提示。
